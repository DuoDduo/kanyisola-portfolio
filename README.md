# Kanyisola Fagbayi

**AI & Machine Learning Engineer and Data Scientist**
Abeokuta, Ogun State, Nigeria

I build machine learning models, AI applications and data platforms, from a maternal health risk model to retrieval-based assistants, fraud and complaint routing systems, and payment infrastructure.

For seven years I have worked inside the Ogun State Government, designing monitoring for social programmes and turning programme data into decisions for senior officials. My work sits where data, AI and public service meet: using evidence and technology to help programmes reach the people they are meant for.

## Projects

- **SENTINNEL Banking:** multi-agent complaint and fraud intelligence. Architect and orchestrator on a nine-person capstone team.
- **SabiLens:** counterfeit product detection for regulators, deployed on Google Cloud. Team capstone.
- **TaxPyre Nigeria:** a plain-language tax law assistant, built as an Agentic RAG system over 500+ pages of legislation. Team project.
- **MamaDey AI:** early maternal risk prediction, my MSc dissertation.
- **Kollectors:** a levy and dues collection platform for estates and cooperative societies, in pre-launch testing. Co-founder and lead engineer.
- **AgroUSSD:** a prototype connecting farmers directly to buyers from basic phones.
